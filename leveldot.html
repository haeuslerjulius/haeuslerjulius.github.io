One dot.
Held in the room.

Move the phone and the dot stays where it was. Roll the phone and it swings around, because a point in the room does not roll with you.

The faint cross is where the phone itself is pointing. When the two coincide, the dot is home. Tap anywhere to recentre.

Axis calibration runs quietly in the background — it locks on by itself once you have happened to roll and tilt the phone a little.
