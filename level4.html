One to one in millimetres, from the accelerometer. Acceleration integrates twice to reach position, and every error compounds on the way — so the integrator continuously forgets. That is the Hold control, and it is the whole trade: long hold gives faithful centimetres and visible creep, short hold gives a solid centre and movements that fade.

Rotation is separate and stays anchored to gravity, so the horizon never drifts.

1.2 s

How long the integrator remembers before leaking back to centre. A real 10 mm move lands around 7–8 mm here; push it to 3 s and you capture 9 mm but a residual bias of 0.02 m/s² creeps 180 mm. Nothing gives you both.

130 %

Compensates for what the leak eats. Above 100 % because a normal-speed move only survives partly — this pulls it back toward true one to one.

0.040 m/s²

Soft gate: acceleration below this integrates as nothing. Raise it if the window wanders while the phone sits still; lower it if gentle movements are ignored.
