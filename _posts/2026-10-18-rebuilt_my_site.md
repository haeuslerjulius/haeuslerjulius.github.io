The old version of this site was a template I filled with posts. It worked, but it didn't show what I actually do.

So I rebuilt it from scratch. Four parts now: me, school, my builds, and my drone work. Plus this log.

Two rules I set before starting:
1) Not 100% AI. AI helps me build and write faster, but the photos are mine, the projects are mine, and nothing goes up here that I haven't checked and stand behind.
2) It has to stay alive. A portfolio that gets updated once and then dies is worse than none. That's why there is a "Right now" strip at the top. If it shows something from two months ago, I failed.

The turnaround at the top is still a placeholder figure. The real version needs 24 photos of me on a tripod, same light, 15° between each. On my list.

What I noticed while building it: deciding what to leave out was harder than building it. Some projects felt too small or too personal to show. That's fine. A portfolio isn't a diary.

For nerds: Jekyll on GitHub Pages, content in a few data files, one file per build. A new project is one Markdown file and some photos.
