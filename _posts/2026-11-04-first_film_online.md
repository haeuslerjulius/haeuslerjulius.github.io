Today my first flythrough film went online. One take, one flight through a house under construction, no cuts.

Uploading it felt like the finish. It wasn't. A film on a CV page tells people who I am. It doesn't tell a client what they get, what it costs or how to book.

So in one evening:
1) A separate page for clients, in German, with the film, what's included and how it works.
2) An Impressum and a privacy page. As soon as you offer a service, the law wants them.
3) A price. I looked at what others charge and went with one product, one price. No packages yet.
4) A list of 10 estate agents and developers around Rosenheim, with the person who actually decides.
5) Ten emails, three lines each, with the link and my WhatsApp.

Then my dad read them and said: they probably get ten of these a day, nobody will answer.
He's probably right. So the emails are only half the plan. The other half is people who know people.

Last time I had a product ready, the review page, it took me two months to walk into the first store. This time the emails go out tomorrow morning.

For nerds: published prices for one FPV flythrough range from about 290€ (a solo pilot in the Ruhr area, basic package) over 490€ (nationwide) to 690€+ (Munich area). Agencies start at 1,500€. I went with 490€, and 340€ as an introductory price for the first jobs.
