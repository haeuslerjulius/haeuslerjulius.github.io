title: "Building My Own Orchestra"

My dad runs his company with a system of AI agents he calls his orchestra. I wanted one too. Not an add-on to his, my own.

The idea is rings around me.
In the middle: me.
Next to me: Personal. That's where my thoughts go, stuff I'm thinking through, decisions. It has no rights to do anything. It just listens.
Then the chief of staff. My right hand. It plans my day, sets priorities and is allowed to act on its own inside a mandate.
Then directors, one for school and one for business. They get small helpers for single tasks when needed.

The mandate was the interesting part. What is it allowed to do without asking me?
For now: no spending, messages only as drafts, my calendar only for blocks that are just for me. Everything else needs my OK.

What it does in practice: every morning a short plan shows up on my phone's home screen. Next exam, what to prep, what to ship this week. On Saturday there's a weekly review.

The school rule I gave it: start studying 7 days before a Klausur. No prep at 5 days left is yellow, at 3 days it's red. I can't ignore my own rule anymore when it's on my home screen every morning.

Let's see if it actually makes me better or just busier. I'll report.
