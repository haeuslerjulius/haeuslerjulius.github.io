Today I turned 18.

Practically, three things changed:
1) I can drive alone now. No one next to me anymore.
2) My screen time is fully my decision. No limits from anyone else.
3) I can sign things myself. Contracts, accounts, a business.

Number two is the one I think about most. A year ago I deleted Snapchat and then Instagram because I didn't trust myself with them. Now there is nobody to blame but me.

I set myself a plan until my 20th birthday. I call it Foundation. Three phases, one per year: learn to think, learn to sell, learn to build. The milestone at the end: earn more than 10,000€ on my own and be able to explain exactly how.

I also rewrote my philosophy last month. The old version, from October last year, was "earn freedom by building systems that work without me." The new one is about building systems and tools around me that make me grow as fast as possible. Competence over comfort. Logic over emotion. Creation over consumption.

The north star stays the same: autonomy.

18 doesn't give me that. It just removes the excuses.
